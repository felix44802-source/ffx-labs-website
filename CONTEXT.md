# Fx Labs Website

The marketing site for Fx Labs, selling AI-powered websites and WhatsApp bots to small business owners. Exists to turn visitors into Leads and Leads into Clients.

## Language

**Service**:
One of the two independently purchasable offerings — the Website Service or the WhatsApp Bot Service. Each can be bought on its own.
_Avoid_: Product, offering, package

**Bundle**:
Both Services sold together at a discounted monthly rate. Conceptually still two Services, just packaged — canceling one does not dissolve the other.
_Avoid_: Combined package, combo, plan

**Illustrative Example**:
A fabricated but plausible result shown on the site to demonstrate what a Service or Bundle could achieve, since no real Client results exist yet. Always visibly labeled as illustrative.
_Avoid_: Case study, success story, testimonial

**Lead**:
A business owner who has initiated contact (via WhatsApp or the contact form) but has not yet purchased anything.
_Avoid_: Prospect, contact, inquiry

**Client**:
Someone who has purchased a Service or the Bundle. None exist yet — this is why Illustrative Examples are needed.
_Avoid_: Customer, user, subscriber
